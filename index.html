技术支持

Qingye Tales · Support for iPhone and iPad

遇到问题？

请邮件联系 heiyuan6@gmail.com。为了方便排查，请附上设备型号、iOS 或 iPadOS 版本、游戏版本和复现步骤。可附上去除个人信息的截图。

存档备份

在游戏内打开「设置 → 导出存档」可保存一份备份。在另一台设备上使用「导入存档」可读取这份备份。卸载游戏前，请先把备份保存到应用之外。

Contact support

Email heiyuan6@gmail.com with your device model, OS version, game version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Remove any personal information from screenshots before sending them.

To back up your farm, export your save from the in-game settings and keep the file outside the app. Use the import option to restore that file.
